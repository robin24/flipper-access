Section: Robin's two decisions, 2026-10-11

Once the user has chosen the card reader, the installer never falls back to USB by itself. A card that does not appear in time ends the install with one sentence that says what to do. This replaces the earlier design, in which the install went on over USB after five minutes.
The card reader way waits for the user to press Enter after each move of the card, to the computer and back to the Flipper, and then looks for it for a short while, and fails with one sentence if it is not there. No clock of five minutes runs while the user moves the card.
