Section: The checks with Robin, 2026-09-30

Robin did everything physical and listened; Claude ran the console. Before the first check the reader's volume was 10: Robin had lowered it with Back and Left, and the settings file differed from the morning's backup in that line alone.

Settings without a card, F2: the other settings stay, but the volume set without a card is not kept, and the fix does nothing on Momentum.
With the card out, the console had no power command (it is one of the commands loaded from the card), and Settings, Power, is an app on the card ("SD card needed. Install SD card. to run this app"). Robin restarted the device with Left and Back held for ten seconds, the hardware's reset, pressing Left first: the reader starts a chord only when a key is pressed while Back is already held (sr_chords.c), so Back and Left in that order is no volume chord.
After that boot without a card the port was /dev/cu.usbmodemflip_R0skie1, the device's name from before the rename, and sr status said "volume: 80", the default, with "vocabulary no". Back and Right made it "volume: 90". storage read /int/.momentum_settings.txt answered "Storage error: filesystem not ready".
The cause: Momentum keeps no internal storage of its own. storage_process_alias in applications/services/storage/storage_processing.c turns every /int path into /ext/.int, a folder on the card. The review's premise for F2, that the settings file is on internal storage, is wrong on Momentum, and the load its fix added at boot without a card (furi/flipper.c, 1f68e0943) finds no file.
