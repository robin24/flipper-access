The sync still looks each clip up; changing it is offered, not done.

Section: The espeak set's gaps

- The clips are alike in length: folder 08's 160 common words last 623 ms on average in samantha, 656 in espeak, 647 in heart.
- Three sentences: heart 0 underruns, espeak 5.
- The time to open each clip, over 25 words: samantha median 23 ms, mean 26, slowest 46; heart median 59, mean 55, slowest 76; espeak median 51, mean 53, slowest 84. espeak's slowest open takes longer than the 82 ms that one half of the speech ring plays, and that is an underrun.
- After the samantha set's rebuild and full sync below: median 23 ms, slowest 43, as before. A sync does not slow the folders down.

The cause lies in the espeak set's files on this card, not in the firmware, which opens samantha's clips in half the time. It is not the lookup: a clip that is there is found as fast in espeak's folders as in samantha's, 25 against 29 ms; only a lookup of a clip that is not there, and a listing, take longer. Where the rest of the time goes is not pinned down. Writing the set's folders fresh might cure it. Not done.

Section: The crash address, F7

Momentum's Crash Test app, a debug app, gained the entry "Bus fault in firmware": strlen reading the address 0x60000000, the external memory bank, which nothing answers on this chip. With the length not volatile, GCC dropped the call, whose result went unused; the disassembly shows the call since. fbt launch copied the app to /ext/apps/Debug/crash_test.fap; the card had no /ext/apps/Debug before.
