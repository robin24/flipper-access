Section: A date with slashes from an NFC card parser

The only parser in the fork that writes a date with slashes is mykey.c: "Prod. date: %02X/%02X/%04X" and, per history entry, "%02d/%02d/%04d %d.%02d". No .nfc dump of an ST25TB card is among the firmware's files, so one was written: a 4K ST25TB with UID D0 02 1C 12 34 56 78 9A, the OTP blocks 0 to 4 at FFFFFFFF, block 8 the production date 15/03/2019 as BCD, an operation count of 42, the history's start at offset 0 and three entries, 02/10/2026 12.50, 15/09/2026 7.80 and 01/08/2026 20.00, then FFFFFFFF; system OTP FEFFFFFF. Sent to /ext/nfc/MyKey_test.nfc, md5 3e351652cbc487a05a239e596366b6e9 on both sides. NFC, Saved, MyKey_test, Info showed the parser's text, scrolled to its end here:
records: 6, content layer: fullscreen
reads as: Current credit: 12.50 euros. Op. history (newest first):. 02/10/2026 12.50. 15/09/2026 7.80. 01/08/2026 20.00. buttons: right More
Robin heard the production date as "15 of 3 of 2019", and found it confusing; a slash heard as "slash" makes more sense, even in a count. The rule was changed so; the reader's code now expands:
Keys found: 5/32 -> Keys found, five slash thirty two
Prod. date: 15/03/2019 -> Prod. date, fifteen slash zero three slash two thousand nineteen
    02/10/2026 12.50 -> zero two slash ten slash two thousand twenty six twelve point five zero
18/100 -> eighteen slash one hundred
